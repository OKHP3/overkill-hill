---
name: Coverage disposition records
description: Keep locale section-gap decisions distinct from route freshness evidence and publication approval.
---

Section-level decisions about intentional abridgments should annotate structural coverage findings, not remove them or count as translation freshness, native approval, or publication acceptance. Bind decisions to the current source and target hashes; stale decisions remain unresolved.

**Why:** A review artifact that repeats route hashes can otherwise look like a duplicate route review, while an accepted abridgment can be mistaken for proof of completeness or permission to publish.

**How to apply:** Keep coverage dispositions explicitly typed and scoped, preserve all reported omissions with their status, and leave the existing noindex and publication boundary unchanged.
